Amazon Break RoomSIMULATOR

Your 15 minutes start now. A free, open-source multiplayer 3D world rebuilt from a real fulfillment center — badge through security, hit the lockers, and meet everyone in the break room for ping pong, giant Connect 4, chess and arcade highscores.

▶  Play now — free, in your browser ⭐ Star on GitHub

No download. No install. Works on desktop, phone and tablet. Guest login in one click.

What's inside

The whole journey of a shift break, recreated room by room from photos of the real building.

🌐

Realtime multiplayer

Everyone shares one world over websockets — chat, emotes, and other players walking, driving and playing live around you.

🏓

Playable minigames

Ping pong duels, giant Connect 4, full chess, and the PRIME BREAKER arcade cabinet with global highscores.

🗺️

Photo-accurate map

Parking lot, badge gates, PAE2 security desk, locker hallway, avenue C market, dining hall, couch corner and the smoke cage.

🚗

Drivable cars

Every car in the parking lot starts. Arcade handling, handbrake slides, synced across players.

🎒

Items & loadout

24-slot inventory and hotbar with drag & drop — snacks, hats, vests, and break-room "weapons" like paddles and bananas.

🕹️

Source-style movement

First or third person, air-strafing, bunny hops, crouch-sneaking — movement that feels like the classics.

🧍

Character customization

Skin, shirt, hair and hats — plus the mandatory hi-vis vest if you want to look like you belong.

🛠️

Live level editor

Admins can drag, rotate and place props in the world — edits persist and appear for everyone instantly.

How to play

Jump in as a guest or make an account to keep your look, items and win record.

InputAction
W A S DMove (hold Shift to run, tap Space to jump / bhop)
EInteract — badge gates, seats, games, vending, car doors
CtrlCrouch / sneak
1–6 / scrollHotbar — equip paddles, snacks and other items
F / clickUse item / swing
Scroll inFirst-person mode (scroll out or 📷 to leave)
📱 TouchVirtual joystick + buttons appear automatically on mobile

Free & open sourceMIT-spirited fan project

The whole game — server, netcode, world builder, minigames — lives in the open on GitHub under the OpenVibers org. Read it, fork it, run your own break room, or send a PR.

  • Zero build step — vanilla JS + Three.js, view-source friendly
  • One-command self-hosting with Docker Compose, or plain node
  • Issues and pull requests welcome — map fixes, minigames, props, anything
